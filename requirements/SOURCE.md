# The requirement list

An exact copy of `docs/backlog/skill-*.yaml` in the design repo, commit 8fcb3fc.
Each item is one requirement, in the PRD's or the owner's words (`quote`, `source`), confirmed by the owner
(`confirmed: true`), with a phase and what a reviewer can check (`accept`). `qa trace-check` maps each item to
its tests. Don't edit these copies: they're exported, and a check fails when they drift.
